Fruit Frenzy

Swap neighboring fruits to line up 3 or more of a kind.

How to play
  • Swipe a fruit, or tap two neighbors, to swap them.
  • Match 4 in a row to make a Blaster. It clears its whole row or column.
  • L and T shapes make a Blaster too.
  • Match 5 in a row to make a Rainbow Bomb. Swap it with any fruit to clear every fruit of that kind.
  • Swap two Blasters for a cross blast, or two Rainbow Bombs to clear the board.
  • Chain reactions multiply your points.
  • Keys: H hint, P pause, M mute.
